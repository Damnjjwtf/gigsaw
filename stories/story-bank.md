## HOW TO USE

1. Read the question category
2. Pick the most relevant story
3. Adapt the framing to the specific role/company
4. Keep it under 2 minutes spoken

## QUESTION CATEGORIES TO COVER

- [ ] Tell me about a time you built something from scratch

- [ ] Tell me about a time you influenced without authority

- [ ] Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision
- [ ] Tell me about your most creative solution to a problem
- [ ] Why do you want to work here?
- [ ] Where do you see AI taking your field in 3 years?
